# Ads and campaigns

How to run saneee.in ads (mainly Meta: Instagram and Facebook) so they look like the brand and not like an ad.

## Principles

1. **One problem, one promise.** Each ad names one pain an owner already feels and the one thing we do about it.
2. **Type-led creative.** A big Saneee Brand headline on ink or lime stops the scroll better than stock photos.
3. **Show real work.** The second-best creative is a real client site on a phone.
4. **No fake urgency, no invented numbers.**
5. **Local.** Mention Mumbai, the area, or the type of business when you target it.

## Formats

| Placement | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feed (IG/FB) | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | Main format |
| Feed square | 1080 × 1080 | Fallback |
| Stories / Reels | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | Keep text out of the top 250px and bottom 400px |
| Carousel | 1080 × 1080 per card | 3–5 cards, one service or one step each |

Keep text on the image short (10 words or fewer for the headline), so it reads at phone size and doesn't look like a flyer.

## Creative templates

**Hook card (insane):** a lime ground, a 2–5 word ink headline, the sigil in the corner.
> *Your website is your first impression.*

**Problem / fix (sane):** an ink ground. Line 1 in paper (the problem), line 2 in lime (the fix).
> Calls, WhatsApps, a notebook.
> **Bookings, in one place.**

**Proof:** a client site on a phone on ink, a short label, the result in plain words.

**Video (6–15s):** the problem on screen as text → a screen recording of the fix → an end card with the wordmark and the CTA.

| Field | Length | Example |

## Campaign structure

A simple setup for a small budget:

```
Campaign: [Objective] · [Service] · [Month YY]     e.g. Leads · Websites · Dec 26
 ├─ Ad set: Mumbai · business owners · 25–55 · [interest or area]
 │    ├─ Ad A: hook card (lime)
 │    ├─ Ad B: problem/fix (ink)
 │    └─ Ad C: proof (client site on phone)
 └─ Ad set: retarget site visitors + IG engagers, 30 days
      └─ Ad D: work carousel + "Tell us what you need"
```

- **Objective:** Messages (WhatsApp or IG DM) for most campaigns, Leads if there's a form, Traffic only for a launch.
- **Test** 2–3 creatives per ad set. Kill losers after about 1,000 impressions each and keep the winner running.
- **Track** messages, calls and enquiries, not likes.
- **Landing:** send traffic to the most relevant page (`/services`, `/work/…` or `/contact`), not always the home page.

## Campaign kit checklist

- [ ] One line: who it's for and the one promise
- [ ] 3 creatives in 4:5 and 9:16
- [ ] Copy within the field lengths above
- [ ] The sigil or wordmark from `assets/`, untouched
- [ ] Prices (if any) written with ₹ in Instrument Sans, not Saneee Brand
- [ ] A matching organic post the same week, so the profile looks alive when people tap through
- [ ] UTM on links: `?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=<name>`
