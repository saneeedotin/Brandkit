## Who we are

**saneee.in** is a web design and software studio in Mumbai. We build websites, custom software, business systems and AI automation for businesses that want to work better and grow.

- **Line:** *Designed to be distinguished.*
- **Two-world line:** *Sanely built. Insanely good.*
- **Call to action:** *Tell us what you need.*
- **Belief:** We don't do templates. Your business isn't generic, so your website shouldn't be either.

### Why the name

Sane on the outside, insane on the inside. The work is calm, clear and easy to use (sane). The craft and effort behind it are over the top (insane). The website shows this literally: a sane paper world and an insane lime world, with a switch between them.

### Who we talk to

Owners and managers of businesses in Mumbai and India: restaurants, cafés, salons, clinics, studios, schools, shops, manufacturers and service firms. They're busy, they aren't technical, and they judge us by the work.

## The rules on one page

**Colour**: Ink `#080808`, Paper `#F6F6EF`, Lime `#C7FF2E`, Grey `#5F5F5A`, plus one team accent per person. No other colours, apart from client work shown inside a frame. Never put lime text on paper. → [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)

**Logo**: Use the files in `assets/`. Paper on ink is the default. Use the sigil (S plus arrow) when the wordmark would be too small. Never retype, stretch or recolour it. → [LOGO.md](LOGO.md)

**Type**: **Saneee Brand** for headlines, labels and buttons. **Instrument Serif Italic** for the voice lines. **Instrument Sans** for long body text. No other fonts. → [TYPOGRAPHY.md](TYPOGRAPHY.md)

**Voice**: Confident, not cocky. Direct, not blunt. Warm, not casual. Short sentences. → [VOICE.md](VOICE.md)

**Shapes**: Buttons and tags are full pills with a 1.5px outline. Images and panels have 4px corners. Use thin 1px rules, never drop shadows.

## Two worlds

| | Sane | Insane |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | Paper, with ink hero and footer | Lime |
| Text | Ink | Ink |
| Selection | Lime highlight, ink text | Ink highlight, lime text |
| Mood | Calm, editorial, trustworthy | Loud, playful, confident |
| Use for | Proposals, decks, print, most posts | Launches, campaigns, covers, ad hooks |

Mix them on purpose. On a feed, a lime post every few posts reads as an event. If every post is lime, none of them stands out.
