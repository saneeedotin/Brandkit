# Voice

Write like one person talking to a business owner across a table. Use short sentences and plain words, with a little dry humour.

## Three traits

| Trait | Means | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Confident, not cocky** | Quiet certainty. The work makes the case. | Superlatives, "best", "leading", "world-class" |
| **Direct, not blunt** | Get to the point. One short word beats three long ones. | Coming across as cold or dismissive |
| **Warm, not casual** | We care about the people we build for, and it shows. | Slang, emoji strings, "fam", forced memes |

## Rules

1. **Talk about them, not us.** Write "Your customers Google you first", not "We are a full-service agency".
2. **Name what they recognise.** Write "bookings", "bills", "customers", "your website". Avoid "solutions", "leverage", "digital transformation", "synergy" and "end-to-end".
3. **Short sentences.** Most are under 12 words. Fragments are fine: "Fast. Clear. Yours."
4. **One idea per line.**
5. **Sentence case and a full stop** on headlines. It sounds calm and sure.
6. **Dry humour, sparingly.** For example: *Who to blame.* (team section), *We read everything. Even the long ones.* (contact form).
7. **No fake urgency.** Don't write "Limited slots!!" or "Act now".
8. **Numbers only if real.** Never invent results, client counts or percentages.
9. **Emoji:** none in designs. In captions, one at most, and arrows (→ ↗) are better.

## Sane vs insane voice

The two worlds use the same facts at a different volume.

- **Sane:** *Websites, custom software and business systems for Mumbai businesses.*
- **Insane:** *Seven things, done properly.*

Insane copy is shorter, more playful and more self-aware. It never turns rude or vague.

## Calls to action, in order of preference

1. Tell us what you need.
2. DM us "WEBSITE" (or another one-word keyword)
3. See the work →
4. Know more →
5. Link in bio

## Spelling

- Use British/Indian English: colour, optimise, centre.
- Write **saneee.in** lowercase, even at the start of a sentence. Never "Saneee", "SANEEE.IN" or "Saneee.in" in running text. The SANEEE caps in the site hero are artwork, not text.
- Use ₹ for prices and the Indian number format (₹1,00,000) when writing for Indian clients.
