## Sizes

| Format | Size | Safe area |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post (preferred) | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | 72px margin all round |
| Square post | 1080 × 1080 | 72px margin |
| Carousel slide | 1080 × 1350 | Same layout on every slide |
| Story / reel | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | Keep text out of the top 250px and the bottom 400px, and 100px clear on the right for the reel buttons |

## Layout templates

**A. Statement (single post)**
```
[label, top-left]        01 / BELIEF
[headline, huge]         We don't do
                         templates.
[sigil, bottom-left]     S↗
```
Ink ground with paper type, or a lime ground with ink type for Belief posts.

**B. Carousel**
- **Slide 1 (cover):** the hook headline, with a label top-left and `Swipe →` bottom-right in brand caps.
- **Slides 2–N:** one point per slide. The page number goes top-right as `02 / 06` in brand caps, in grey or at 60% opacity. The headline is in Brand, with up to 3 lines of support in Sans.
- **Last slide:** a serif voice line plus the CTA pill ("Tell us what you need"), with the wordmark at the bottom.

**C. Work showcase**
- Ink ground, the client's site in a 4px-corner frame or a phone mock.
- Label: `WORK / CLIENT NAME`. Headline: the result in plain words ("Bookings moved online.").
- Only use real numbers that the client approves.

**D. Quote / People**
- Paper ground. The quote in serif italic, 56–72px. The name and role in brand caps below. A real photo if there is one.

## Reels

- **Length:** 7–30 seconds. The hook comes in the first second, as on-screen text in Saneee Brand.
- **Structure:** hook (1–2s) → show (the work, the process, the screen) → one line of payoff → end card.
- **End card (1.5–2s):** the wordmark on ink, or the sigil on lime, plus "Tell us what you need" or "www.saneee.in".
- **Captions:** burn them in. Use Saneee Brand at 52–64px, paper on an ink pill or ink on a lime pill, one line at a time, centred inside the safe area.
- **Motion:** quick cuts on the beat, with the type sliding up and settling (fast start, soft landing). The sigil animation goes at the start or end only.
- **Screen recordings:** record at 1080 wide, zoom in on the detail, and let the lime cursor point at things.
- **Sound:** clean and modern. No trending audio that clashes with the calm sane look, except on insane reels.

## Don't

- Don't use Canva templates, stock photos or AI-generated people.
- Don't flood a slide with lime or put lime text on paper.
- Don't use more than one serif moment per slide.
- Don't invent results or put fake testimonials up.
- Don't use emoji in the design.
